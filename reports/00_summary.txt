Aim: to quantify how extreme, in statistical terms, the sixth-century drought
recorded by Hoti Cave stalagmite H12 (Fleitmann et al. 2022) is, and to
identify which parts of that claim are robust.


Answers
-------
1. Magnitude. The 520-525 CE anomaly of 3.43 permil is the largest value in
2630 yr, 5.1 standard deviations above the rest of the record and 1.3 permil
above the next independent dry peak. A 30-yr window carries most of the excess
kurtosis of the whole record.

2. Persistence. It sits inside a 70-yr spell (475 to 545 CE), ranked first in
peak and second in severity and duration among 26 spells.

3. Rarity with the event in the fit. Threshold and block models agree on a
return period of roughly 5,562 to 20,766 yr across 36 analysis choices (MLE
11,594 yr). No choice brings it below the record length.

4. Rarity judged by the rest of the record. Leaving the window out, the data
alone bound the dry tail near 2.1 permil, below the event. With a neutral
prior on the tail shape, 60 % of posterior draws put the event beyond the
tail. With a heavy-tail geophysical prior the median is 9,138 yr. The tail
shape, which 19 peaks cannot resolve, controls the answer.

5. Local frame. Against the climate state of its own century the event still
ranks first (residual 3.24 permil), so it is not a by-product of a drier
regime.

6. Timing. The +/-30 yr age uncertainty overlaps the Aksumite conquest and the
decline of Himyar but cannot single them out.


Bottom line
-----------
The H12 record supports a record-breaking, multi-millennial drought in the
early sixth century CE. The statistical evidence for its magnitude is strong
and robust; its exact return period is uncertain by an order of magnitude; its
link to Himyar rests on timing that the dating cannot resolve below a few
decades.


Caveats
-------
Single stalagmite; d18O only (no d13C); no age-model ensemble; the transcribed
table should be checked against the original NOAA file; isotopes are a proxy
for winter-spring rainfall, not rainfall itself; 19 to 21 declustered peaks
limit every tail model.
