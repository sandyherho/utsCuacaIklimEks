Interpretation
--------------
Three statements follow. First, judged by the data alone, the rest of the
record has a bounded dry tail ending near 2.1 permil, and the sixth-century
value exceeds every other independent dry extreme by 1.3 permil. In that sense
the event lies outside the behavior of the stalagmite over the other twenty-
five centuries.

Second, that conclusion depends on the tail shape, which 19 peaks cannot pin
down. Under a neutral uniform prior on xi, 60 % of posterior draws without the
window place the event beyond the upper limit of the tail, and the chance of
such an event in a record of this length is 8 %. Under the heavy-tail Martins-
Stedinger prior it is 9,138 yr (versus 4,374 yr with the event included), and
that chance rises to about 29 %. The prior on the tail shape, not the data,
decides between 'outside the normal range' and 'rare but expected once in a
few such records'.

Third, every variant agrees that removing the event lengthens its return
period, which confirms that fitting a model to the event itself flatters it.
The defensible summary is a record-breaking, multi-millennial drought whose
exact return period is uncertain by an order of magnitude or more, not a
precise recurrence time.
