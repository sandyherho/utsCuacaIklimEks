Interpretation
--------------
With the event included, the GPD places the 520-525 CE anomaly at a return
period of about 11,594 yr, roughly 4 times the length of the record. The
bootstrap interval is wide because only 19 independent peaks constrain the
tail, and its upper end includes resamples whose bounded tail cannot reach the
event at all. The point estimate is also optimistic for a subtle reason: the
event itself is one of the peaks used for fitting, which lengthens the fitted
tail toward it. Step 7 removes this circularity.
