Interpretation
--------------
Median anomalies differ between epochs: -0.45, 0.12 and -0.78 permil for E1
(631 BCE-479 CE), E2 (480-1399 CE) and E3 (1400-1996 CE). The middle epoch is
the driest, consistent with the persistently low effective moisture reported
for about 480 to 1400 CE. The record is therefore not stationary in its mean,
which matters for the extreme value models in steps 5 to 8.

The 520-525 CE bin lies 5.1 standard deviations above the mean of the
remaining record and is the largest value on the grid. Positive skewness and
excess kurtosis indicate a heavier dry tail than a Gaussian, and the normal
quantile plot shows the event as the single point furthest from the line.

Removing the six bins of 500-530 CE lowers the excess kurtosis from 1.23 to
0.21 and the skewness from 0.69 to 0.45. A 30-yr window, about 1 % of the
record, therefore carries most of the heavy-tail signature of the full
distribution.
